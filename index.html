The 2026 Rasuwa–Bhotekoshi Flood

In early September 2026, a catastrophic flood surge traversed over 100 km down the Bhotekoshi–Trishuli corridor in central Nepal, claiming over 1,350 lives and severing a tenth of the national power grid.

Core scientific finding: This was an ice–rock avalanche that dammed and burst a river, NOT a classic Glacial Lake Outburst Flood (GLOF). Rapid Himalayan warming doubled ice loss rates since 2000, destabilizing the high-altitude cirque.
